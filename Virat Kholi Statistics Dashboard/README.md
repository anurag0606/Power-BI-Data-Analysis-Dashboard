## 🧩 Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a **comprehensive analysis of Virat Kohli's cricket career**, covering performance across ODI, T20I, and Test formats.  

It highlights key batting metrics such as runs per year, average, strike rate, 4s, 6s, 50s, 100s, and includes format-based filtering for deeper insights.

The dashboard is designed for cricket analysts, fans, and data enthusiasts who want to explore Kohli’s legendary cricketing journey interactively.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Cricket analysts and fans often struggle to view a complete career analysis of a player in one place.  
This dashboard answers questions like:

- How has Virat Kohli performed year-by-year?  
- What is his scoring trend in major formats (ODI, T20I, Test)?  
- How many 50s and 100s has he scored each year?  
- What is the distribution of his 4s and 6s?  
- What is his average and strike rate over time?  

This dashboard consolidates all these insights in a single interactive platform.

## 🧹 Data Preparation

- Removed missing and inconsistent entries  
- Created calculated columns for:  
  - Strike Rate  
  - Average  
  - Boundary %  
- Grouped data by Year & Format  
- Created DAX measures for totals and trends  
- Added slicers for:
  - Year Range  
  - Format (ODI, T20I, Test)

## 📈 Key Findings

1. Virat Kohli shows a strong upward trend in total runs between 2012–2018.  
2. Significant peak years observed in 2016 and 2017.  
3. Strike rate remains consistently strong across formats.  
4. Majority of his boundaries are **4s (97%)**, with **6s contributing the remaining percentage**.  
5. The dashboard reflects consistent ability to score big in all three formats.
