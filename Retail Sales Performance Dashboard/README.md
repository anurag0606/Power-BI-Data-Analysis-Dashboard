# 📊 Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

_An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail sales
performance, customer behavior, product trends, and budget achievement._

## 📌 Overview

This dashboard analyzes retail sales, customers, products, budgets, and
regional performance using interactive Power BI visuals.

## 🎯 Business Problem

This dashboard helps answer: - Are sales meeting budget? - Which
customers generate the highest sales? - Which products perform best? -
Which cities contribute the most revenue? - How do monthly sales compare
with budget?

## 📁 Dataset

- Order Date
- Customer
- Customer City
- Product
- Category
- Sub Category
- Sales
- Budget

## 📈 Key Insights

- Bikes contribute the largest share of sales.
- Mountain bike products are top sellers.
- Sales exceed budget in several periods.
- A few customers contribute a major share of revenue.
